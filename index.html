Charity CRM Systems: Insights from Customer Reviews (2026)

An independent study by Alice Nguyen exploring public customer reviews of nine systems used to manage donors, fundraising and memberships.

9 platforms, 7 assessment themes

What I examined and how

I collected review excerpts from Capterra and G2 to compare customer experiences of nine systems used for charity, fundraising and membership work.

The systems were Salesforce Nonprofit Cloud, Blackbaud Raiser's Edge NXT, Beacon, CharityEngine, Spektrix, iMIS, Access Charity CRM, Blackbaud eTapestry and Donorbox. Donorbox was included as a donation-tool comparison.

I organised the collected evidence around seven themes and assigned scores from 0 to 2. Higher scores indicate a more favourable assessment in five themes and greater concern in the two risk themes. The three bands bring together the scores and findings from the review excerpts, rather than simply adding the scores.

This is a small, self-directed study based on public review data. The findings describe the customer accounts collected, with scoring and grouping involving judgement. They provide a starting point for comparison.

What stood out in the reviews

  • Flexibility can take more learning. Some reviewers valued customisation while finding everyday tasks difficult.
  • Ask about getting started. Reports of data transfers, bulk uploads and duplicate records highlight questions about setup and support.
  • Check the terms as well as the features. The eTapestry excerpts raised concerns about contract renewals alongside reporting.
