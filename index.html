Charity CRM Systems: Insights from Customer Reviews (2026)

An independent study by Alice Nguyen exploring public customer reviews of nine systems used to manage donors, fundraising and memberships.

9 platforms, 7 assessment themes

What I investigated and how

What can customer reviews tell us about choosing a charity CRM? I explored nine platforms on Capterra and G2, looking at how people described managing donor and member records. Donorbox was included as a donation tool comparison.

The systems covered are Salesforce Nonprofit Cloud, Blackbaud Raiser's Edge NXT, Beacon, CharityEngine, Spektrix, iMIS, Access Charity CRM, Blackbaud eTapestry and Donorbox.

I organised the review excerpts around seven themes, from everyday usability and support to data transfers and reporting. A simple 0-2 scoring scale helped me compare them and arrange the findings into three bands.

It’s a self-directed study based on public reviews. The scores reflect my interpretation, and the bands are a starting point for comparison, not a final verdict on each platform.

What stood out in the reviews

  • More features can take more learning. Some reviewers valued flexibility but found everyday tasks difficult. Others praised ease of use. Both matter when choosing a system.
  • Getting started matters too. Data transfers, bulk uploads and duplicate records featured in the accounts I read. These are useful questions to bring to a product demonstration.
  • Look beyond the software. The eTapestry reviews raised concerns about contract renewals as well as reporting. Choosing a platform also means understanding the terms that come with it.
